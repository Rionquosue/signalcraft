Compact, not crowded

System type. 14 px body and scheduling data, 12 px metadata. 4 px spacing grid. 10 px cards and 8 px controls. Visible focus and comfortable touch targets.

Control stays explicit

Pause is per account and shown on the account, not each card. Reordering previews changed times. An uncertain result offers status checks before any manual retry.

Small screens stay useful

One account queue or chronological list. Full-page composer. Stacked schedule rows. Every drag action has a keyboard alternative.
